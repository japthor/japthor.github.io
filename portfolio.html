Skyline Bowling

A hybrid-casual bowling game built around quick sessions, simple drag controls and satisfying physics.

I worked on live updates, gameplay changes, SDK integrations, bug fixing, performance optimisation and build-size reduction. I also helped set up and track A/B tests for KPI-driven product iterations.

Derailed: Survival Adventure

A hybrid-casual survival adventure where players drive a train through dangerous landscapes, gather resources, recruit a crew and make decisions along the way.

I focused on performance work, build-size reduction, bug fixing, SDK integrations, new gameplay features and A/B test support.

Oil Digging 3D

A hybrid-casual game about building and running an oil operation, from drilling the first well to creating a pipeline network for transport.

Tower Smash

Sharpshooter Blitz

Hospital Simulator 3D

Wheel Race
